## Priority Photos To Take

### 1. Sunday Arrival Hero

Purpose: Homepage and Plan Your Visit hero.

Shoot:

- Families walking toward the main entrance.
- Greeters near the door.
- The exterior entrance clearly visible.
- Warm natural light if possible.
- A mix of ages and life stages.

Avoid:

- Empty building-only photos.
- Tight crops where visitors cannot understand where to go.
- Photos that feel staged or formal.

## 2. Worship Gathering

Purpose: Explain simple Church of Christ worship.

Shoot:

- Congregation singing together.
- A view that shows people and the room.
- Communion table or worship elements if naturally visible.
- Open Bibles or songbooks if present.

Avoid:

- Stage-performance emphasis.
- Dark concert-style images.
- Photos that focus only on the preacher.

## 3. Kids Bible Class

Purpose: Build confidence for visiting families.

Shoot:

- Children in age-appropriate Bible class.
- Volunteers helping children.
- Check-in or classroom entrance if available.
- Parents being welcomed or guided.

Avoid:

- Photos without adult supervision visible.
- Chaotic or cluttered classroom shots.
- Posting identifiable children without proper permission.

## 4. Fellowship Lobby

Purpose: Show warmth, belonging, and real church life.

Shoot:

- People talking after worship.
- Coffee/fellowship moments.
- Newer families being greeted.
- Multi-generational conversations.

Avoid:

- Posed group photos only.
- Photos where the room feels empty.
- Crops that make people look isolated.

## 5. Bible Study And Prayer

Purpose: Show Scripture-centered community.

Shoot:

- Adults around tables with open Bibles.
- A small group praying.
- Intergenerational class discussion.
- Warm classroom lighting and real participation.

Avoid:

- Photos that feel like a lecture only.
- Empty chairs, empty podiums, or empty classrooms.

## 6. Leadership Photos

Purpose: Help visitors recognize shepherds and ministers.

Shoot:

- Consistent portraits of elders and ministers.
- Natural light if possible.
- Warm, approachable expressions.
- Similar crop and background for all leaders.

Avoid:

- Mixed lighting and inconsistent crop sizes.
- Old or low-resolution photos.
- Overly formal headshots if they feel distant.

## 7. Local Community Photos

Purpose: Root the site in Fulshear/Katy.

Shoot:

- Exterior church sign and surrounding area.
- Local service projects.
- Events with Fulshear/Katy neighbors.
- Meals, outreach, first responders, school partnerships, or community service.

Avoid:

- Generic stock-looking community images.
- Photos that do not clearly connect to this church or local area.

## Practical Photo Notes

- Shoot horizontal images for website sections.
- Leave negative space in some photos for text overlays.
- Capture both wide shots and close human moments.
- Use natural light when possible.
- Prefer real interaction over posed smiles.
- Get permission before using photos of children.
- Keep original high-resolution files.
